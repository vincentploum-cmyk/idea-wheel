# Projected minutes: the line and the power plays (2026-11-07)

Vincent, 2026-11-07: *can we predict how many minutes skaters are projected to get based on potential power plays
(opponent allows a lot of powerplays) and the line he is playing? Line 4 gets fewer minutes than line 1.*

Measured on every regular-season skater-game of 2025-26 and of 2026-27 to November 6 (56,445 skater-games, 3,136
team-games), from the NHL stats API's game-by-game reports (`tools/audit/nhl-toi-fetch.sh`): each skater's minutes
split even strength / power play / short-handed, each team's power plays and kills. Every read is built from the games
before the date; the fits are made on 2025-26 and scored unchanged on 2026-27. The script is `tools/audit/nhl_toi.py`;
every table quoted is in [2026-11-07-nhl-toi-tables.md](2026-11-07-nhl-toi-tables.md).

The public box score carries no lines, so the line is read two ways: **projected** (the rank of his last-10
even-strength minutes among the skaters his team dressed that night, forwards in threes, defencemen in pairs) and
**as played** (that night's even-strength rank). The second comes from the result itself, so what it earns is a
ceiling on what a posted lineup could earn. The site's posted lines (the `line` on the stored skater rows) sit
somewhere between the two.

## In one paragraph

**The line: yes, it matters a lot.** A first-line forward plays 19.4 minutes and a fourth-liner 10.5; a first-pair
defenceman 23.2 and a third-pair one 14.9. Most of that is already in his own recent minutes, which is why a slot
read from those minutes adds nothing. A posted lineup adds something only when it moves him. Skaters who played a
slot lower than their recent minutes said averaged 15.7 minutes against a last 10 of 17.6; skaters who moved up
averaged 17.3 against 15.5. Knowing the played line cuts the miss from 1.94 to 1.56 minutes a skater (−20%). That is
the ceiling, and the posted lines will earn less of it. **The power plays: no, not before the game.** In hindsight
each extra power play is worth about one power-play minute to a first-unit skater, but he gives back 0.56 at even
strength, so a first-unit forward nets +0.39 minutes a power play and a skater off the power play loses a little.
Before the game, the two teams' penalty rates foresee a power-play count that ranges only from 2.7 to 3.3 (5th to
95th percentile) and correlates 0.1 with what happens: it misses by exactly as much as the league average does.
Across that range a first-unit forward should gain about 0.2 minutes, and the data shows none: added to the line
and his power-play role, the matchup's power plays move the miss from 1.511 to 1.510.

## What the model does now

`projectedToiFromLineup` in `components/nhl/model-core.js` takes the larger of his last-5 minutes and a floor by
tonight's line (F 18.5 / 16 / 13.5 / 11, D 21 / 18.5 / 16). The result, `effectiveToi`, gates the role tags and
probability floors (`buildDefenseRoleMarketBoost`, the goal-leak overrides) and scales the assist base. The shots λ
itself does not scale with it. Measured:

- **It runs high.** It projects +0.6 minutes over what is played with the projected slot and +0.9 with the slot as
  played, because it only lifts. 2026-27 miss: 2.09 / 1.91 minutes against 1.97 for his last 10.
- **It never lowers a demoted skater.** Skaters moved down a slot played 15.7 minutes. The rule said 17.6, his own
  number, because the floor is below it.
- It lifts the promoted ones, a little past what they played: 17.7 against 17.3.

## What would read better (not shipped)

On 2025-26, with the played line, the best plain fit is:

`toi ≈ −1.10 + 0.11 · L10 + 0.24 · L5 + 0.64 · (the line's mean minutes) + 2.70 · PP share + 2.76 · PK share`

- The line's mean minutes are F1 19.4, F2 16.7, F3 14.1, F4 10.5, D1 23.2, D2 20.0, D3 14.9.
- The PP and PK shares are his share of the team's power-play and penalty-kill minutes over his last 10.
- On 2026-27 it misses by 1.51 minutes, with no bias. It follows the line changes: 15.8 for the demoted (played 15.7)
  and 17.4 for the promoted (played 17.3).

The matchup's power plays are left out because they measured nothing.

It is not shipped, for three reasons:

1. The line coefficient was fitted on the played line, which comes from the result, so it is too large. It needs
   refitting on the site's stored posted lines (`line` on the skater rows) against the minutes played.
2. `effectiveToi`'s thresholds (tags at 14 / 15 / 17 / 21 minutes and so on) were set against the current rule's
   higher figures. Lowering the figure moves skaters across them.
3. The model's math is changed only with Vincent's call, and then ported into `App.jsx`
   (`docs/claude/nhl.md`, "Porting model changes").
