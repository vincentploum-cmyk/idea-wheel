Volume and share lean a few points the right way on some lists and not on others, inside the noise of 180 picks; on every
rung at once (a logistic on the said chance and one feature, fitted 2024–25, log loss on 2026) his volume and share added
nothing to receiving (+0.0) or rushing (−1.1, −5.8 to +3.6). Sorting by the rung's own chance is a trap: the rungs that say
77–90% are the ones whose spread reads narrowest, and they land 10 points under (a narrow spread is partly noise).

## What shipped

**The rungs on a curve of their own** (`RESID_POS`, `RANGE_POS`, `residFor` in `lib/nfl/projections.js`): a back's receiving
yards, a back's rushing and a quarterback's rushing read their group's curve for the ladder (strong / lean / thin) and the
floor / ceiling; everyone else the pool's. Through the site's own replay after the change (said → landed):

| | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A back's receiving, strong / lean / thin | 67→63 · 51→49 · 34→37 | 66→63 · 50→49 · 34→32 | 66→61 · 50→51 · 34→34 |
| A back's rushing | 67→61 · 51→48 · 35→33 | 66→68 · 50→51 · 35→36 | 66→62 · 50→49 · 35→34 |
| A quarterback's rushing | 67→69 · 51→54 · 35→36 | 67→66 · 51→49 · 34→35 | 67→59 · 51→42 · 35→30 |

Floor / ceiling (under the floor / over the ceiling, 20% each by design): a back's receiving 21 / 21%, 17 / 18%, 21 / 23%; a
back's rushing 23 / 20%, 16 / 20%, 21 / 20%.

**The pick rule, as measured**: a play is a rung at its stated chance; the lists' order is as good a way to read them as any
tested, and none beats it. A strong rung is a 60–67% play on every list now, lean 45–55%, thin 30–37%, touchdowns as said.
Do not sort by the rung's chance. The outliers keep the pool's curve (their own calibration was fitted on it).
