# The NBA: can PropFinder's odds help find opportunities?

Vincent, 2026-11-13: "check whether the odds from propfinder can help us find opportunities". The first test of the board
against real prices: the site's own stored nights, every ladder rung the pull carried a price for, with the board's chance
and what the player did (`GET /api/nba/scorecard?rows=1` through the `nba-odds-export` workflow; `tools/audit/nba_odds.py`,
tables in `2026-11-13-nba-odds-tables.md`). 16,473 priced lines, 23 nights (Oct 20 – Nov 12), 324 players.

## What the stored odds are

- **Overs only, one book a line.** No stored rung carries an under price (`underOdds` is empty on all 134,871) and `books`
  is 1 at most. So an under cannot be graded, and there is no price to shop.
- **The over prices are steep.** The median main line is the over at about −165 (points 9.5 at −148, rebounds 3.5 at −172,
  threes 1.5 at −167). The price implies 61.3%; the over landed 51.6%.
- **557 early rungs carry a price that is not theirs** (Durant over 2.5 points at +180: a first-quarter or first-minutes line
  filed under the game's, fixed in the reader on 2026-10-30). They are left out; left in they read as a 90% edge.

## Answers

1. **Betting the overs blind loses 16% a unit**, and every price band loses (−5% near even, −23% at −400 and shorter). Every
   market loses: points and PRA −13%, rebounds −12%, assists −14%, threes and steals −19%, blocks −21%.
2. **The board's edge sorts the bets, and almost none of them win.** Return a unit by the board's chance less the price's:

   | Edge | bets | landed | return | halves |
   | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
   | under −15 points | 5,869 | 49.4% | −22.5% | −18.6 / −26.6% |
   | −15 to −8 | 4,652 | 50.2% | −16.6% | −15.3 / −17.8% |
   | −8 to −3 | 3,071 | 53.5% | −11.6% | −7.6 / −14.4% |
   | −3 to +3 | 2,095 | 55.5% | −7.9% | −9.6 / −6.7% |
   | +3 to +8 | 552 | 56.3% | −2.8% | −0.9 / −4.5% |
   | +8 to +15 | 168 | 63.1% | **+6.7%** | +5.0 / +8.3% |
   | over +15 | 66 | 71.2% | **+21.6%** | +14.6 / +27.4% |

   The order is right at every step, which says the edge is information. Only an edge of 8+ points returned money: 234
   bets in 23 nights (ten a night), both halves, with a 90% range that still reaches zero. Too few to call proven.
3. **The other side is where the board and the price disagree most, and it cannot be bet from this data.** On 5,869 lines
   the board put the over 15+ points under the price; the price said 64%, the over landed 49%. Whether the under there pays
   depends on the under's price, which the pull does not hold.
4. **The price knows things the board does not.** Reading one line, the board and the price (its hold taken off) score the
   same (Brier 0.2400 and 0.2413); half and half 0.2357. Fitted together the weights are 0.42 on the board and 0.40 on the
   price. By market the price leads on points, rebounds and PRA (0.58–0.68 against 0.11–0.26); the board leads on threes,
   steals and assists (0.54–0.57 against 0.22–0.39).
5. **The book's line is a better projection than ours for points and PRA.** Where the main line's over is priced −160 to
   +130: points, the board missed by 5.00 and the line by 4.84 (1,334 player-nights); PRA 6.40 and 6.16 (1,989). Half and
   half: 4.86 and 6.19 — 2.7% and 3.2% better than the board, 90% ranges 1.9–3.5% and 2.3–4.2%, both halves of the nights.
   For rebounds, assists, threes, steals and blocks a half-point line is too coarse: the board is as good or better.

## Shipped

- **`lineBlend`** (`LINE_BLEND` in `lib/nba/picks.js`, the cocktail's): the points and PRA centre moves half way to the
  book's main line where its over is priced −160 to +130; the ladder is built again on it, so the range, the rungs and the
  shuffle follow, and the card's hover names both figures (`ranges[market].lineRead`). Read again off the live site after
  the deploy, every priced main line: points 4.926 → 4.850 (54% of them moved), PRA 6.347 → 6.186 (79%); Brier at the main
  line 0.2484 → 0.2444 and 0.2554 → 0.2512. `buildBoard({ lineBlend: false })` reads the board without it.
- The scorecard's rows carry the under's price, the book count, the projection and what he got on every rung; the
  `nba-odds-export` workflow writes them as an artifact.

## What would find opportunities

- **Under prices and more than one book in the pull.** The largest disagreement is on the under side and there is no price
  to grade it; one book leaves nothing to shop. PropFinder's item carries them or it does not: the pull's `fieldsSeen` is
  where to look (not done here).
- **A list of the overs at an 8+ point edge** is the one cut that paid. Ten a night; it needs another month before it is
  more than a lead.
- Not done: the price blended into the chance the board prints (answer 4). It would make the chances more honest and the
  edges smaller; it is a change to what "edge" means on the page, so it is Vincent's call.
