# The stat lists' top 10: where the misses come from (2026-11-24)

Vincent, 2026-11-24: "In the points, assists, rebounds and threes views, we need to see how we can reduce the number of
misses in the top 10. See if any patterns can be detected to increase the accuracy for each one of them."

**What is graded.** The Points / Rebounds / Assists / Threes tabs (`NbaStatList` → `tierRows`) list the night by projection;
each row shows the highest round mark he clears at 70% (strong), 55% (lean) and 40% (thin) on the shuffle's chance
(`tierBoard`). A miss is a mark shown in red once the game is in. Here: the night's ten highest projections per stat, every
mark they show.

**Data.** The board replayed (`npm run audit:nba … --marks-all`: the site's `buildBoard` and shuffle on a pull rebuilt from
ESPN's logs, pseudo lines, the box score's starters standing in for RotoWire) over 2025-26 (160 nights, the learned trees
off, since the shipped trees were fitted on that season) and this season to 2026-11-23 (30 nights, the trees on). 20,184
marks. ESPN's pickcenter for the spread. Cross-checked on the site's own graded rungs (the daily-results artifacts,
2026-11-16 to 11-23). Script: `tools/audit/nba_top10.py`; tables: [2026-11-24-nba-top10-tables.md](2026-11-24-nba-top10-tables.md).

## The answer: most misses are the chance playing out; one pattern holds, the back-to-back

- **The marks land at the chance they state.** Strong said 75–80%, hit 76–82%; lean said 60–63%, hit 59–64%; thin said
  45–48%, hit 43–49% — every stat, both seasons, within 3 points (the tables' first block). A 70% mark misses 3 nights in 10
  by design.
- **A night's misses, per stat, in the top 10** (2025-26 / this season): strong 2.0–2.4, lean 2.4–3.8, thin 3.4–5.6. Points
  ~8 misses a night, threes ~9, assists and rebounds ~11. **The thin column is half of them** (a 40–50% mark).
- **Half the misses come with lost minutes.** He played under 90% of the minutes read on 52% (2025-26) and 50% (this season)
  of the missed marks; under 75% of the read, the marks hit 32–36%. The minutes read for a regular (28+ minutes) runs 2.2–2.4
  minutes high in both seasons, 3.4–3.6 for a 35–38 minute read and 3.1–3.2 when tonight's read sits 2+ above his last ten;
  his rate a minute makes up for it (the projection carries no bias), so this is where the spread lives, not a bias to take
  out. Games with a spread of 12+: 47% / 38% of the regulars lose 4+ minutes, against 31% / 29% in close games.

## Per stat: what separates the misses, beyond the chance stated

One read at a time over the stated chance (hit = σ(a + b·logit(said) + c·z(read))), then a candidate fitted on 2025-26 and
read unchanged on this season. Only a read with the same sign and weight in both seasons is called a pattern.

| Stat | Holds in both seasons | Looked like it, did not hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assists | **Back-to-back**: strong marks 68.5% (n 270) and 68.6% (n 51) on the second night against 80.0% / 80.3% on the others; every tier 5.5 / 10 points under the chance stated | matchup factor (over-applied in 2025-26, not this season), sd |
| Rebounds | Back-to-back, smaller: strong 73.4% / 69.6% against 76.9% / 77.9% | the highest minutes reads (−2.0 / −7.3 points), hot last five (2025-26 only) |
| Points | nothing of weight: the back-to-back is −1.6 / −4.2 on every tier, the strong marks the same either way (79% / 82%) | big underdog (spread +6.5 or more: −1.4 / −11.4), hot last five (−4.5 / +0.1), matchup factor |
| Threes | nothing | big underdog (−8.2 / 0.0), back-to-back (−1.9 / −5.3) |

Leaving the rest out on purpose: his sd on the stat, his share of his last ten at the mark, the game total, the
environment and team-scale factors, place on the list and games played each moved one season and not the other; folded into
a calibration with the back-to-back they made this season's Brier worse.

**Why the board missed the back-to-back.** The cocktail tested rest on every player and found nothing (`lib/nba/picks.js`):
pooled, it cancels. Actual over projected, by the third of the night's projections (2025-26 / this season):

| Stat | top third, rested | top third, 2nd night | bottom third, rested | bottom third, 2nd night |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Assists | 1.032 / 0.988 | 0.982 / 0.972 | 1.061 / 0.949 | 1.116 / 1.113 |
| Rebounds | 0.997 / 0.986 | 0.965 / 0.971 | 1.019 / 0.992 | 1.073 / 0.998 |
| Points | 1.003 / 0.991 | 0.984 / 0.965 | 1.001 / 0.926 | 1.064 / 1.068 |
| Threes | 0.978 / 1.004 | 0.986 / 0.971 | 1.048 / 0.981 | 1.100 / 1.072 |

The regulars give 2–5% back on the second night and the bench takes it. Their minutes on that night fall about a minute
further under the read (regulars 28+: 2.8 against 2.3 minutes short in 2025-26, 2.7 against 2.1 this season).

**The back-to-back as a shift on the chance** (logit p + c on the second night, c fitted on 2025-26, the tiers re-read on
this season's 30 nights):

| Stat | odds × | Brier (every height) | misses / marks shown | 2nd-night marks hit |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Assists | 0.82 | 0.11405 → 0.11353 | 338 / 883 → 326 / 878 | 51.0% → 57.5% |
| Rebounds | 0.95 | 0.10540 → 0.10538 | 344 / 896 → 340 / 895 | 54.8% → 56.9% |
| Points | 0.95 | 0.11230 → 0.11226 | 241 / 703 → 240 / 706 | 61.3% → 62.6% |
| Threes | 0.91 | 0.12711 → 0.12690 | 260 / 729 → 259 / 726 | 60.3% → 60.1% |

Assists is the one worth a change: 12 fewer misses over 30 nights, the second-night rows from 51% to 57.5%, the marks a step
lower on those rows only. The rest are noise-level.

## The site's own nights (2026-11-16 to 11-23)

The same top-10 read off the stored rungs the scorecard graded (round marks where a rung sits on one): strong assists said
76% hit 82% (71), threes 78% / 73% (79), rebounds 75% / 66% (58); lean threes 61% / 50% (44). Eight nights, 11–79 marks a
cell: the rebounds and threes gaps are within noise of the replay's, worth re-reading once the artifacts hold a month.
