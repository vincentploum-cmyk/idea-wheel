# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
One person — Vincent — on NHL game days. Opens the site in the late afternoon and evening,
often on a phone between things and on a 1440px desktop when working the slate. The job:
in under a minute, see which skaters have a good matchup for shots and goals tonight, check
a player's form and history, and know that the data behind it is complete. Dense numbers
are the content; nothing is marketing.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, confident. A well-set analyst's notebook, not a product: quiet surfaces,
strong hierarchy, generous space around dense tables. Numbers are the hero; colour is a
signal, never decoration. Emotional goal: "I trust this, and I can read it fast at night."

### Aesthetic Direction
- References: PropFinder (colour-coded hit rates, per-game histograms) for content;
  MoneyPuck (table-first, utilitarian) for restraint.
- Anti-references: the generic SaaS template it started from (rounded cards everywhere,
  violet-to-blue, icon-above-heading grids); sportsbook / betting-app energy (neon green,
  flashing odds, promo tone); BI dashboard KPI tiles.
- Theme: dark by default (evening, phone in a dim room), light available. Both derived
  from ice: cold, slightly blue-tinted neutrals (OKLCH hue ~235–250, chroma 0.01–0.02).
- Colour: the rink supplies the only chromatic cues — rink blue for interactive/selected,
  rink red for danger/stingy defence, and desaturated green only where "permissive /
  over the line" needs to read at a glance. Hot = warm ember, cold = ice blue.
- Type: Schibsted Grotesk (display/headings — precise, editorial, Nordic) with Source Sans 3
  (UI/body — calm, precise, no slashed zero in tables). Tabular numerals in every table.
  No Outfit, no Inter.
- Shape: small radii (6–10px), hairline borders, no drop shadows on dark, no nested cards.
  Sections separate with space and rules, not boxes.

### Design Principles
1. Numbers first — every screen leads with the table or figure, not a heading or a pill.
2. Colour means something — green/red/ember/ice only ever encode a state; never used as paint.
3. Flat and quiet — one surface level per view; no card-in-card, no glow, no gradient.
4. Same thing looks the same everywhere — one rank cell, one form chip, one table style
   across Teams, Matchups, League and the player card.
5. Readable at night on a phone — 15px minimum body, +0.05 line-height on dark, 65ch max.
